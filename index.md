## 🛠️ Frequently Asked Questions

### Where do I find my sound library?
The software prompts you to download the sound library upon the first launch. Ensure your internet connection remains stable during this process.

### Does the software support MIDI keyboards?
Yes. Plug your MIDI controller into your computer via USB. The software recognizes standard USB controllers automatically. If your controller uses 5-pin MIDI cables, connect them to your audio interface.

### How do I update to newer versions?
Launch the application and select Help from the top menu. Click Check for Updates. The software tells you if a newer version exists and provides a link to download it.

### Can I run this on older versions of Windows?
This version targets Windows 10 and Windows 11. Older versions like Windows 7 or 8 might experience compatibility issues, so we do not recommend them. 

### My interface does not show up in the list.
Check your device cables. Reinstall the driver for your specific audio interface provided by its manufacturer. Close the software and restart it once you install the latest drivers.
